Free Fire is optimized for low-end Android devices. With 10-minute matches and a 50-player lobby, it rewards fast reactions and precise aiming more than most mobile shooters. The right settings can cut your headshot time by seconds.

## H2: DPI Settings for Free Fire

### H3: What DPI Means on Mobile

DPI on mobile works differently than PC. Most Android devices run at **standard DPI** (between 400-480 DPI equivalent). Tweaking sensitivity is the equivalent of adjusting DPI on PC.

### H3: Pro Sensitivity Reference

### H3: Classic Pro Button Layout

Free Fire allows extensive customization. Here's the recommended layout:

| Button | Position | Action |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Fire (left thumb) | Bottom-left corner | Continuous fire |
| Aim (right thumb) | Bottom-right, next to fire | ADS |
| Crouch | Left side | Quick crouch-jump |
| Prone | Left side, below crouch | Fast prone |
| Jump | Right side | Mobility |
| Reload | Auto or left side | Never lose fire button |

### H3: The "No Thumb" Crouch Trick

**Setting:** Set crouch to **tap** (not hold)
**Benefit:** You can fire + crouch simultaneously for headshots. This is the single most impactful mechanical change for headshot rate.

## H2: Headshot Settings — The Advanced Approach

### H3: The Headshot Formula

```
Headshot Rate = (Aim Accuracy × Sensitivity Consistency × Head Hitbox Positioning)
```

**Key insight:** Headshots in Free Fire come from:
1. **Timing** — firing at the right moment (not spray-and-pray)
2. **Positioning** — aiming at head level before engaging
3. **Sensitivity** — consistent micro-adjustments

### H3: Quick Headshot Tips

- **Always scope in first** — scoped shots have tighter spread
- **Pre-aim corners** at head level before peeking
- **Crouch-tap** (not hold) for the fastest headshot
- **Practice in Training Mode** daily for 10 minutes
- **Use red dot** for close range, 4x for medium

## H2: FAQ

**Q: What sensitivity do Free Fire pro players use?**
A: Most pros use General sensitivity between 85-100 with lower scope sensitivity (60-75) for precision.

**Q: Does Free Fire have aim assist on mobile?**
A: Yes. Free Fire has **auto-aim assist** that activates when you're close to an enemy. You cannot turn it off. This makes headshots easier than PC shooters.

**Q: Is 60 FPS or 90 FPS better in Free Fire?**
A: 90 FPS is better if your phone can sustain it without overheating. If you drop below 30 FPS at 90 FPS mode, switch to 60 FPS for consistency.
