## H2: What Is Satisfactory? Game Overview

Satisfactory is a first-person factory-building game where you explore an alien planet, gather resources, and build massive automated production lines. Unlike factorio, Satisfactory is 3D and emphasizes spatial planning and visual satisfaction.

**Core loop:** Explore → Extract → Automate → Expand → Optimize

## H2: Your First 30 Minutes — Getting Started

### H3: Initial Priorities (Order of Operations)

1. **Scan 5 Caterium Ore nodes** — unlocks Quickwire (critical early component)
2. **Gather enough Iron Ore** to craft your first Storage Container
3. **Build aconstructor** — produces Iron Ingots automatically
4. **Build a Smelter** — converts ore into ingots

### H3: Key Early Buildings

| Building | Function | Unlock |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Miner | Extracts resources from nodes | Free (starting) |
| Smelter | Smelts ore → ingots | Free |
| Constructor | Converts ingots → parts | Free |
| Assembler | Complex parts (e.g., motors) | Tier 2 |
| Storage Container | Stores up to 100 stacks | Free |

## H2: Biomass — Your First Power Source

Before you unlock Coal Power, you'll need **Biomass Burners**.

### H3: Biomass Recipe Priority

| Biomass Type | Burn Time | Yield/Node | Best For |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Leaves** | 4 sec | 10/min | Very early game only |
| **Wood** | 8 sec | 15/min | Early game, plentiful |
| **Mycelia** | 8 sec | 20/min | Early-mid game |
| **Biofuel** | 12 sec | 50/min | Most efficient biomass |
| **Solid Biofuel** | 30 sec | 100/min | Before coal power |

### H3: How to Make Solid Biofuel (Most Efficient)

```
1. Gather Wood (harvest trees)
2. Feed Wood to Constructor → Biomass
3. Feed Biomass to Constructor → Solid Biofuel
4. Route Solid Biofuel to Biomass Burner
```

This gives you **8× more power** than burning raw wood.

## H2: Factory Layout Principles

Satisfactory is all about **spatial planning**. Poor layouts will slow you down for hundreds of hours.

### H3: The "Main Bus" Concept

Inspired by Factorio, a **main bus** is a central corridor with all your resource lines running parallel.

```
[ Iron Plates ] ←→ [ Copper Plates ] ←→ [ Iron Rods ]
     ↓                  ↓                   ↓
[ Screws ] ←——————— [ Reinforced Plates ]
     ↓
[ Modular Frames ]
```

**Pro tip:** Build your main bus **at ground level** and build upward (z-axis) as you expand.

### H3: The "Madness" Layout (No-Planning Approach)

Early game, most players just build what's closest. This works until mid-game when spaghetti belts become unmanageable.

**Tip:** You can **demolish** anything (free). Don't be afraid to tear down and rebuild.

## H2: Production Chain Basics

### H3: Essential Production Chains for Beginners

**Iron Plate Line:**
```
1× Iron Ingot (from Smelter) → 2× Iron Plate (from Constructor)
```

**Copper Ingot → Wire Line:**
```
1× Copper Ingot → 2× Wire (Constructor)
Wire × 3 → 1× Copper Sheet (Constructor)
```

**Reinforced Iron Plate Chain:**
```
Iron Plate × 2 → 1× Screw (×4 in Constructor)
Screw × 12 + Iron Plate × 2 → 1× Reinforced Iron Plate
```

## H2: The MAM — Unlocking Better Recipes

The **MAM (Mutual Assimilation Machine)** lets you unlock **Alternate Recipes** that make production more efficient.

### H3: Top 3 Most Important Alternate Recipes

| Recipe | Normal → Alternate | Benefit |
|--------|-------------------|---------|
| **Copper Alloy Ingot** | 1× Copper + 1× Iron | Saves Copper ore |
| **Iron Alloy Ingot** | 1× Iron + 1× Copper | Saves Iron ore |
| **Heavy Modular Frame** | Alternate recipe | 75% less Iron Ore required |

## H2: FAQ

**Q: Can I play Satisfactory solo?**
A: Yes, fully playable solo. Multiplayer is available but not required.

**Q: What's the endgame in Satisfactory?**
A: There is no traditional "win" state. The goal is to reach all tiers, optimize your factory, and complete the **Project Assembly** (story/milestone system).

**Q: Should I use blueprints or build manually?**
A: For beginners, build manually to learn the game. Once you understand layouts, use the Blueprint Designer to copy-paste sections.

**Q: Does Satisfactory save automatically?**
A: Yes. Auto-save every 30 seconds. Manual saves available in the menu.
