## H2: What Is Rust? A Quick Overview

Rust is a multiplayer survival game where players gather resources, craft weapons, build bases, and fight for survival on a hostile island. Unlike single-player games, every player you see can be a threat or an ally. The core loop: **gather → craft → build → defend → raid**.

## H2: Choosing Your Spawn Point Wisely

Your first decision matters more than you think. Avoid beaches — they are high-traffic combat zones.

### H3: Best Starting Areas for Beginners

| Area | Pros | Cons |
|------|------|------|
| **Littoral (beach)** | Fast scrap collection | PvP-heavy, high risk |
| **Small monuments** | Blue cards, recycler access | Requires some gear |
| **Sparse forests** | Hidden, low traffic | Slower loot progression |
| **Domes (Aqua/D道)** | High-tier loot | Requires keycards |

**Recommendation:** Start in a **sparse inland area**. Collect stone, wood, and cloth quietly. Move toward monuments only after you have a basic bow.

## H2: Resource Gathering — Your First Hour

### H3: What to Collect First (Priority Order)

1. **Wood** — 500+ for basic tools and furnace
2. **Stone** — 300+ for weapons and tools
3. **Hemp (cloth)** — 100+ for clothing and medical
4. **Metal Ore** — 200+ for tools after unlocking furnace

### H3: Key Crafting Recipes for Day 1

| Item | Resources | Use |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Pickaxe | 200 wood, 100 stone | Mining efficiency |
| Hatchet | 150 wood, 100 stone | Wood gathering |
| Hunting Bow | 100 wood, 50 cloth | Self-defense |
| Arrow (×10) | 100 wood | Ammunition |
| Stone Spear | 150 wood, 75 stone | Early PvE defense |
| Sleeping Bag | 30 cloth | Respawn point |
| Furnace | 300 stone | Smelting ore |

### H3: The "Crucial First Tool" Hack

> **Pro tip:** Before upgrading your pickaxe, spend the first 10 minutes gathering **scrap**. Early recycler monuments (Gas Station, Supermarket) give free scrap even without a kit.

## H2: Building Your First Base — The Mini-Scouter

Do NOT build a big base on day one. You will get raided.

### H3: The Classic 2x1 Layout

```
[ Furnace ] [ Sleeping Bag ×2 ]
[    TC (Tool Cupboard)      ]
[    Storage Box             ]
```

**Why this works:**
- TC coverage: ensure the TC covers all walls (check with building plan)
- 2 foundations wide = enough room for essentials
- Easy to defend with a single door

### H3: Building Tips for New Players

- **Upgrade to sheet metal** before placing anything valuable
- **Hide your base** — don't build near obvious landmarks
- **Double-door rule** — always use 2 doors on your entrance (airlock)
- **Raid protection:** Cover every wall with your TC's radius

### H3: Common Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Consequence | Solution |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Building near monuments | Attracts raiders | Build 200m+ away |
| Leaving doors open | Easy loot loss | Close every door |
| Storing all loot in one box | One raid = total loss | Spread loot across boxes |
| Building too big too fast | Huge upkeep cost | Start small, expand slowly |

## H2: Understanding the Day/Night Cycle

- **Day (0:00–12:00):** Safer for gathering, visibility is high
- **Night (12:00–24:00):** Naked players are nearly invisible. Watch out for ambushes. Use a torch sparingly.
- **Tip:** Carry a torch only when necessary — it makes you a target.

## H2: PvE Enemies — Muraderers and Scientists

### H3: Muraderers
- Patrol certain monuments at night
- Deal high damage if you're under-geared
- **Strategy:** Run past or wait for them to patrol away

### H3: Scientists
- Found at research stations and monuments
- Drop medical supplies and mid-tier weapons
- **Strategy:** Use doors to LOS (line of sight) them; shoot from cover

## H2: Next Steps — Your First Week in Rust

Once you've survived your first 24 hours:

1. **Start stockpiling scrap** — you'll need it for research and upgrades
2. **Find a Recycler** — Gas Station or Supermarket monuments
3. **Craft better weapons** — SAR (半自动步枪) is the best early-game rifle
4. **Join a clan** — survival is much easier with teammates
5. **Plan your raids** — only raid when you can afford the loss

## H2: FAQ

**Q: How do I get started in Rust without dying immediately?**
A: Play solo on a low-population server at off-peak hours. Stay away from other players until you have basic gear. Use your map knowledge to avoid combat zones.

**Q: What server type is best for beginners?**
A: vanilla or noob-friendly servers. Avoid community servers with higher rates unless you want an casual experience.

**Q: Can I play Rust offline?**
A: Rust is an online-only game. You cannot play offline.
