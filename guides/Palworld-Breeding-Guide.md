## H2: How Breeding Works in Palworld

Breeding in Palworld is one of the most powerful systems in the game. By breeding two Pals, you can pass on traits and potentially create a Pal with better stats than either parent.

### H3: Unlocking the Breeding System

1. Reach **Technology Level 15**
2. Unlock the **Breeding Farm** technology (costs 10,000 Gold)
3. Place the Breeding Farm in your base
4. Assign two Pals of the same species or different species to the farm

### H3: Breeding Rules

- **Same-species breeding:** Produces the same Pal with combined parent stats
- **Cross-species breeding:** Produces a new Pal based on the parents' species combination
- **Breeding interval:** ~30 in-game minutes between breeding cycles
- **Egg time:** Hatching takes 5-30 minutes depending on the result

### H3: The Breeding Chart

Palworld uses a species combination system. Every Pal can be bred with every other Pal, producing a specific offspring. Use community resources like the **Palworld Breeding Calculator** to find combinations.

> **Note:** Breeding calculator sites (community tools, free to use):
> - https://palworld.net/breeding-calculator

## H2: Speed Breeding — Fastest Pals

Speed is critical for resource gathering and exploration.

### H3: Best Speed Combos

**Primary:** Jormuntide Drake (S-rank speed, great stats)
**Alternative:** Direhowl Quivern (B-rank but fast + good carrying capacity)

**How to breed Jormuntide Drake:**
- Parent 1: Vanwyrmin (found in snowy biomes)
- Parent 2: Fulgur (found in desert areas)
- Result: Jormuntide Drake

### H3: Speed Stat Tips

- Breed Pals with **Speed IV passives** for maximum effect
- Passives can be inherited — if a parent has "Swift" passive, the offspring has a chance to inherit it
- **Lucky Pals** (gold-bordered) have enhanced base stats — always breed from Lucky parents when possible

## H2: Attack Breeding — Best Combat Pals

### H3: The Best Combat Breed: Anubis

Anubis has the highest **Work Attacker** bonus in the game. It can mine ore, fight, and guard your base simultaneously.

**Breed Anubis:**
- Parent 1: Univolt
- Parent 2: Gobfin
- Result: Anubis

### H3: Best Pure Combat Breed: Elizabee

Elizabee is a **Legendary-tier** combat Pal that can be bred.

**Breed Elizabee:**
- Parent 1: Queen Bee (Mothive)
- Parent 2: Kingpaca
- Result: Elizabee

> **Tip:** Both parents must be bred from base-spawned Pals — you cannot breed bosses.

## H2: How to Hatch Eggs Faster

### H3: Egg Temperature System

Pal eggs need the right temperature to hatch:

| Egg Type | Temperature | Solution |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| Red egg | Hot | Place near furnace or heater Pal |
| Blue egg | Cold | Place in snow biome or Icebreaker Pal |
| Purple egg | Normal | Most environments work |

### H3: Hatching Tips

- Put eggs in a **Dedicated Breeding Base** with temperature-control Pals
- Keep the base at **Day 3 speed** (Pald Time ×3) — eggs hatch in ~8-12 minutes with this setting
- Check egg color immediately after breeding to know what conditions you need

## H2: FAQ

**Q: Can you breed Legendary Pals in Palworld?**
A: Yes, but not bosses. You can breed legendary-tier Pals like Anubis, Elizabee, and Jormuntide Drake through specific parent combinations.

**Q: How do I get better stats on bred Pals?**
A: Breed from parents with high stats. If both parents have 4-star stats, the offspring will too. Lucky Pals are the best parents.

**Q: What is the rarest Pal to breed?**
A: Jormuntide Drake and Elizabee are among the rarest to breed legitimately. Most players obtain them through breeding combos found on community calculators.
